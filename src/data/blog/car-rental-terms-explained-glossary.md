## Quick Answer
Understanding common rental terms like "Mileage Allowance," "Like-for-Like Fuel," "Self-Drive," and "Chauffeur Rental" helps you compare options transparently and avoid unexpected misunderstandings.

## Key Takeaways
* Rental Day usually refers to a 24-hour cycle or calendar-day period as agreed.
* Chauffeur services include driver charges but usually exclude fuel and toll taxes unless specified.
* Self-drive requires identity validation and clear fuel/return terms.
* Always clarify definitions with 4WHEELS before signing any agreement.

## Key Car Rental Jargon Demystified

### 1. Self-Drive
Renting a car where you or an approved designated driver operates the vehicle without a hired chauffeur.

### 2. Chauffeur-Driven
Renting a vehicle accompanied by a professional driver employed by the rental company.

### 3. Rental Cycle / Rental Day
The agreed period representing a single day's rental. This can be configured as a 24-hour clock cycle or a fixed calendar day.

### 4. Security Deposit
An amount or guarantee provided to cover potential damages, traffic fines, or unreturned fuel during self-drive usage.

### 5. Mileage Allowance
The maximum distance (in kilometers) permitted during the rental duration. Excess kilometers beyond this cap may incur additional per-km charges.

### 6. Fuel Policy: Like-for-Like
A policy where the customer returns the vehicle with the exact same level of fuel present at pickup.

### 7. Outstation Travel
Driving the rental vehicle outside the city limits of Lahore (e.g., traveling to Islamabad, Faisalabad, or Multan).
