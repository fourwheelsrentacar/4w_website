## Quick Answer
Drive cautiously in Lahore, utilize navigation apps like Google Maps, respect speed limits on Ring Road and Canal Road, and use paid official parking lots in commercial markets.

## Key Takeaways

* Use designated parking plazas in Gulberg, Johar Town, and DHA.
* Keep CNIC and driving license accessible.
