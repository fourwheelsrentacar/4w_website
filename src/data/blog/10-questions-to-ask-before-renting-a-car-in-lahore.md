## Quick Answer
Before renting a car in Lahore, ask about exact daily time calculations, fuel policies, driver accommodation rules for outstation trips, mileage allowances, and official payment channels.

## Key Takeaways
* Clarify exact pickup and drop-off timing expectations.
* Ask whether fuel, motorway toll taxes, and driver food allowances are included or separate.
* Confirm emergency assistance contact procedures.
* Verify the official business location and communication numbers.

## 10 Vital Questions for Every Rental Customer

Asking clear questions before booking protects you against surprises and ensures a smooth trip.

1. **How is a rental day defined?** (Is it a strict 24-hour period or based on calendar day returns?)
2. **Is fuel included or strictly like-for-like?**
3. **What documents do I need to bring for verification?**
4. **Is outstation travel permitted with this specific vehicle?**
5. **What is the mileage allowance per day?**
6. **For driver-driven rentals, who handles driver food and stay on outstation trips?**
7. **What is the exact official payment channel?**
8. **What is the procedure if I experience an unexpected delay or need to extend?**
9. **How are pre-existing damages recorded during handover?**
10. **What is the dedicated contact number for helpline or trip updates?**
