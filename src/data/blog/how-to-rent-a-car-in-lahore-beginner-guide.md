## 1. Decide Between Self-Drive and Chauffeur-Driven Rentals

When renting a car in Lahore, your first major decision is choosing whether you want to drive the vehicle yourself or hire a professional chauffeur.

* **Self-Drive:** Ideal if you value personal privacy, flexible schedules, and have experience navigating busy Pakistani urban traffic and roads.
* **With-Driver:** Recommended if you are visiting Lahore for business, family trips, or want to avoid parking hassles and dense traffic management.

## 4. Documentation and Identity Verification

Before a car is handed over, standard identity and verification procedures must be completed. Requirements vary depending on self-drive or chauffeur services:

### What to prepare for verification:
* Valid original CNIC or Passport
* Valid Pakistani driving licence or International Driving Permit (for self-drive)
* Verified local contact and address details

## 5. Pre-Departure Vehicle Inspection

Never drive away from the rental location without conducting a thorough vehicle inspection:

* **Exterior Bodywork:** Check and record existing scratches or dents on camera.
* **Tyre Inspection:** Check tread depth and inflation pressure.
* **Cabin & Controls:** Test air conditioning, lights, wipers, and horn.
* **Fuel & Odometer:** Note fuel gauge level and current mileage reading.

## Questions to Ask Before Booking

2. Is fuel included or managed on a like-for-like return basis?
3. What is the process if my trip duration needs to be extended?
