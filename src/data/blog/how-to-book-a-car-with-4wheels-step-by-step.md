## The Official 4WHEELS Booking Process

4WHEELS Rent a Car maintains a streamlined, direct communication booking flow designed for speed, clarity, and safety.

### Step 1: Initiate Inquiry via Official Channels
Contact 4WHEELS directly using our single official phone and WhatsApp number:
* **Phone / WhatsApp:** **0321 6616644**
* **International WhatsApp:** **+92 321 6616644**

### Step 2: Provide Journey Details
When contacting our team, share the following details for a fast and accurate response:
1. **Service Type:** Self-drive or with professional driver.
2. **Vehicle Category:** Economy, Sedan, or SUV.
3. **Dates & Duration:** Start date, pickup time, and expected return date.
4. **Destination:** City travel within Lahore or outstation travel.

### Step 3: Availability & Vehicle Confirmation
Our team checks vehicle schedules and provides immediate confirmation regarding model availability, condition, and applicable terms.

### Step 4: Identity Verification
For security and compliance, submit requested verification documents (CNIC / Passport and Driving Licence) via WhatsApp or in person at our Johar Town office.

### Step 5: Vehicle Handover & Inspection
Upon confirmation, collect your vehicle from **5 F-1, Block F1, Johar Town, Lahore** or arrange agreed delivery. Complete a physical walkaround inspection before taking the keys.
