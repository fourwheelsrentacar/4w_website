## Quick Answer
After requesting a rental car, the rental company verifies vehicle availability, validates driver documentation, confirms booking terms, conducts a joint physical vehicle inspection, and issues the keys for handover.

## Key Takeaways
* Requests trigger availability checks across fleet management systems.
* Document submission is validated prior to contract finalization.
* Pre-rental inspection ensures pre-existing vehicle state is recorded.
* Clear agreements are made regarding fuel and return schedules.

## The Journey of a Car Rental Request

Understanding what happens behind the scenes after you place a car rental request ensures a smooth, seamless handover experience.

1. **Inquiry Request** -> 2. **Availability Check** -> 3. **Document Verification** -> 4. **Agreement & Inspection** -> 5. **Vehicle Handover** -> 6. **Return**

## Step 1: Availability & Fleet Checking
When your request is received, staff cross-reference fleet schedules, maintenance routines, and turnaround times to ensure a clean, roadworthy vehicle is ready.

## Step 2: Customer Identity Verification
Document details are reviewed to comply with identity verification standards and safety protocols.

## Step 3: Terms and Policy Alignment
Key parameters are explicitly confirmed with you:
* Exact pickup and drop-off schedule.
* Fuel management expectation (return with same level).
* Allowed geographical boundaries (city use vs outstation).

## Step 4: Joint Vehicle Walkaround
Before departure, both customer and representative inspect the vehicle together, noting exterior condition, odometer reading, and fuel levels.
