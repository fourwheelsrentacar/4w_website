## Quick Answer
To rent a car for self-drive in Lahore, rental companies usually require a valid original CNIC or Passport, an active Pakistani driving license or International Driving Permit, and emergency contact details. Chauffeur services generally require simpler identity verification.

## Key Takeaways
* Self-drive rentals mandate valid, unexpired driver identification and licensing.
* Overseas visitors require a valid Passport and recognized International Driving Permit for self-drive.
* Always clarify exact document requirements with 4WHEELS prior to booking.
* Never hand over original documents to unverified third parties.

## Essential Verification Requirements for Car Rental

Verification protects both the rental service provider and the customer. Requirements vary based on service type.

## 1. Requirements for Self-Drive Rental

For self-drive rentals, you must prove legal eligibility to drive on Pakistani roads and verify your identity:

* **National Identity Card:** Original Computerized National Identity Card (CNIC) for resident citizens.
* **Driving Licence:** Original, unexpired Pakistani driving licence.
* **For Overseas Pakistanis & Foreign Tourists:** Original Passport with valid visa entry stamp and an International Driving Permit (IDP).
* **Proof of Address / Reference:** Secondary contact details or utility bill reference if requested by the management.

## 2. Requirements for Chauffeur-Driven Rental

Because a professional driver handles the vehicle, document checks are simplified:
* Original CNIC or Passport copy for identity verification.
* Primary contact phone number and pickup location details.
