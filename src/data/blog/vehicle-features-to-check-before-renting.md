## Quick Answer
Before driving off, check AC cooling speed, headlight/indicator function, power window controls, seatbelt locking, brake responsiveness, and spare tyre kit.

## Key Takeaways
* Test AC cooling power in warm weather.
* Verify all lights and signal indicators work.
* Ensure spare tyre is inflated and jack is present.
