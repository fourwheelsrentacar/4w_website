## Quick Answer
Inspect a rental car by walking around the exterior in daylight, capturing photos/videos of existing scratches or dents, checking tyre tread and air pressure, testing the air conditioning and lights, and noting fuel gauge levels.

## Key Takeaways
* Take high-resolution video and photos of the entire vehicle exterior during handover.
* Check tyre condition and verify presence of spare wheel and jack.
* Test essential cabin controls including AC, signals, wipers, and horn.
* Ensure the current fuel level is mutually documented.

## The Pre-Rental Vehicle Inspection Walkaround

Taking 5 minutes to conduct a vehicle walkaround protects you when returning the vehicle.

## 1. Exterior Walkaround & Video Recording
Walk completely around the car with your phone camera running. Pay special attention to:
* Front and rear bumper corners.
* Side mirror housings.
* Door edges and lower side sills.
* Windscreen glass for existing chips or cracks.

## 2. Tyre and Wheel Safety Check
* **Tread & Sidewalls:** Ensure tyres are not worn smooth and show no sidewall bulges.
* **Spare Tyre & Tool Kit:** Confirm the spare tyre, jack, and wheel nut spanner are present in the boot.

## 3. Interior & Comfort Features
* **Air Conditioning:** Switch on the AC and verify cooling efficiency (crucial for Lahore summers).
* **Dashboard Warning Lights:** Turn the ignition on and check that no engine or oil warnings remain lit.
* **Cabin Cleanliness:** Confirm upholstery cleanliness and seatbelt functionality.

## 4. Odometer and Fuel Gauge Verification
Take a clear photo of the instrument cluster showing both the total odometer reading and current fuel gauge level.
