## 4. Guidance for Brand Protection & Lookalike Disclaimer Wording

### Approved Public Disclaimer Style:
> *"Other businesses and digital properties in Pakistan or online may use similar '4 Wheels', '4Wheels', or '4 Wheel Rent a Car' naming. Similar naming does not establish affiliation with 4WHEELS Rent a Car. Please verify any claimed branch, franchise, representative, or payment instruction directly with official 4WHEELS Rent a Car customer care at 0321 6616644 before completing a transaction."*

### Legal & Tone Rules:
1. **No Public Accusations**: Never accuse third parties of fraud, crime, or theft without documentary and legal review.
2. **Focus on Verification**: Frame messages around protecting the customer and providing official contact channels.
3. **Keep Sales Pages Positive**: Confine disclaimer notices to official verification hubs (`/official/`, `/press/`) and relevant footer links. Commercial pages should remain positive and conversion-focused.
