## 2. Fleet vs. Catalog Distinction Rule

1. **4WHEELS Core Fleet:**
   - Vehicles owned or directly managed by 4WHEELS Rent a Car with confirmed availability and physical photography.
   - Distinctly labeled as `4WHEELS Core Fleet` or `Check Current Availability`.

2. **Pakistan Vehicle Catalog:**
   - Official vehicle models sold or launched in Pakistan that customers can inquire about or request.
   - Distinctly labeled as `Pakistan Vehicle Catalog` or `Request This Model`.
   - Never presented as company-owned property; 4WHEELS performs custom availability checks upon request.
