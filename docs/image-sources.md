# 4WHEELS Vehicle Image Sources & Attribution Registry

This document registers all photography, illustrations, and visual media used across the 4WHEELS Rent a Car platform.

---

## 1. Primary Image Priority Strategy

For every 4WHEELS fleet vehicle, visual representation adheres strictly to this priority order:

1. **ACTUAL 4WHEELS-OWNED VEHICLE PHOTO**
   *Label:* `Actual 4WHEELS vehicle`
   *Condition:* Used only when verified as genuine photography of a vehicle in the 4WHEELS Lahore fleet.
2. **LICENSE-COMPATIBLE REAL PHOTO OF EXACT MODEL / GENERATION**
   *Label:* `Representative model image. Actual rental vehicle/color may vary.`
   *Condition:* License-compatible (CC0, Public Domain, CC BY, CC BY-SA) real photograph matching verified model year, generation, trim, and body style.
3. **MODEL-ACCURATE ORIGINAL 2D ILLUSTRATION**
   *Label:* `Model illustration. Actual rental vehicle/color may vary.`
   *Condition:* Vector artwork capturing headlight geometry, roofline, wheelbase proportions, and body shape of the verified vehicle generation.
4. **BODY-TYPE / CATEGORY FALLBACK**
   *Label:* `Temporary representative visual. Actual vehicle may differ.`
5. **GENERIC AUTOMOTIVE SILHOUETTE**
   *Emergency fallback only.*

## 3. Owner Photo Request List (Photos Needed from 4WHEELS Owner)

To upgrade representative photography to **Actual 4WHEELS Vehicle Photos** on the live website, the following original photographs are requested from the 4WHEELS fleet manager / owner:

### 1. Toyota Corolla (Altis / Grandi / GLi)
- **Angles required:** Front 3/4 exterior, Rear 3/4 exterior, Driver interior dashboard.
- **Notes:** High-resolution outdoor daytime photo showing current clean bodywork.

### 2. Honda Civic (Oriel / RS Turbo)
- **Angles required:** Front 3/4 exterior, Cockpit/Center console interior.
- **Notes:** Front 3/4 preferred in natural sunlight.

### 3. Toyota Fortuner (Sigma 4 / Legender)
- **Angles required:** Front 3/4 exterior, Rear 3-row seating interior view.
- **Notes:** Highlighting VIP status and 7-passenger capability.

### 4. Toyota Hilux Revo (Rocco / V)
- **Angles required:** Side profile / Front 3/4, Open rear bed / interior.

### 5. Suzuki Alto (660cc VXL / VXR)
- **Angles required:** Front 3/4 exterior, Trunk / boot luggage fit view.

### 6. Toyota Yaris (Ativ)
- **Angles required:** Front 3/4 exterior, Rear seat legroom.

### 7. Toyota HiAce (Grand Cabin 13-Seater)
- **Angles required:** Exterior high-roof side view, Interior 13-seat passenger aisle view.

### 8. Toyota Coaster (Executive 22-Seater) — **HIGH PRIORITY**
- **Angles required:** Exterior side/front profile, Customized 22-seat Saloon interior layout, Rear luggage space.
- **Notes:** Crucial to distinguish customized 22-seat luxury executive layout from factory 29-seat standard coaster.

### 9. Audi A6 (1.8 TFSI Executive) — **HIGH PRIORITY**
- **Angles required:** Front 3/4 exterior showing exact front grille and headlight design, Rear executive seating area.
